# Brand and date aware shipped-units reporting

**Country:** Usa\
**Brands:** SM, DV, ATM\
**Status:** Reports delivered; dashboard permissions updated\
**Contribution:** Investigation, validation, and stakeholder coordination

## Issue and business impact

Operations needed store-level revenue and units for fiscal and calendar periods and a reusable way to obtain future extracts.

## Expected behavior and requirement

Allow brand, store, and custom date selection while separating store shipments from warehouse-only activity.

## RCA and evidence

The request combined data scope with usability: later users could not access the dashboard or change dates. My follow-up reported permission updates rather than treating report delivery alone as completion.

## Documented actions

1. I delivered shipped-units workbooks for SM, DV, and ATM with the requested date periods.
2. I explained store-total exclusions and that the inspected ATM periods contained no store shipments.
3. I shared the reusable dashboard and later reported updating permissions for access and date filtering.

## Outcome and remaining work

Report delivery and the permission change are documented. Final user confirmation of the revised dashboard access is not included.

## Recommended validation and prevention

The following checks are proposed follow-up guidance unless explicitly confirmed above.

- Test a viewer’s ability to change dates and export without author permissions.
- Reconcile store totals against warehouse exclusions.
- Display inclusive period boundaries.

## Evidence basis

My October 9 report delivery and October 29 access update in “Shipped Units By Store.”
