# Exclude recalled facility supply from online preorders

**Country:** Canada\
**Brands:** SM, DV\
**Status:** DV change reported live; SM rollout still pending\
**Contribution:** Investigation workstream participant; team rollout reference

## Issue and business impact

Canada preorder availability included on-order units from a recalled-stock location, creating unintended online promises.

## Expected behavior and requirement

Use eligible incoming supply for preorder publication and exclude recalled stock from the designated facility.

## RCA and evidence

The thread identified the need for explicit facility exclusion. A later correction narrowed an earlier deployment message: the change was live for DV, while SM still needed its rollout. That correction governs this case’s status.

## Documented actions

1. I was included in the issue review and stakeholder follow-ups.
2. The team developed and tested support for excluding specific facilities.
3. The latest clarification reported DV deployed and SM planned for the following week.
4. The business manually removed preorder settings on an example and planned a later verification.

## Outcome and remaining work

Partial rollout is documented. Neither both-brand completion nor the later verification should be inferred from the earlier deployment message.

## Recommended validation and prevention

The following checks are proposed follow-up guidance unless explicitly confirmed above.

- Test excluded and eligible facility supply independently for both brands.
- Check upcoming POs and existing storefront preorder flags.
- Record per-brand rollout and acceptance dates.

## Evidence basis

“Dolce Vita Canada - preorders pulling from on order units,” September 25–October 29.
