# Review scope and evidence rules

This edition was curated on November 2, 2026. It expands the original selected portfolio using Gmail conversations and accessible Codex chats.

The outgoing-mail discovery returned 183 messages and was fully paginated after excluding common employee/access subjects. Broader ATM, BJ, Betsey, and Dolce Vita reply searches returned 127 messages and were fully paginated. In total, 84 distinct relevant/supporting mail threads were read, alongside 27 NEC notification messages and selected Codex investigations. Search-result populations overlap and must not be summed as unique incident counts. Relevant conversations were then read with a maximum of thirty recent messages per thread. Some related work appears only in forwarded quoted history. Subject filters, thread limits, archived chats, missing attachments, and inaccessible systems mean this is not a claim that every mailbox message or every historical incident has been examined.

Codex evidence includes investigations of customer-number mapping, report STYLE values, duplicate order creation, preorder mapping timing, inventory-channel eligibility, historical order import, split PO lines, and daily call summaries. A prepared draft is not a sent email. A proposed action is not an executed fix. An assistant explanation without direct evidence is treated as a hypothesis or historical investigation finding.

Each case names a sanitized source thread or chat. The public repository omits raw mail, private contact details, order/customer identifiers, production payloads, database endpoints, credentials, and private log links. Routine employee provisioning is excluded. Similar label and queue requests are grouped to avoid counting each order as a separate engineering achievement.

Brand indexes identify confirmed case scope. A shared case is not automatically applicable to every brand in the country matrix. Where the source does not establish a brand, it is explicitly marked unconfirmed. Team fixes retain team attribution.
