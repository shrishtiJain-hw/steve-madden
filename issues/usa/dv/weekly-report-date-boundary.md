# Weekly shipped-units report used the wrong dates

**Country:** Usa\
**Brands:** DV\
**Status:** Corrected report delivered\
**Contribution:** Investigation, validation, and stakeholder coordination

## Issue and business impact

A weekly report used September 1–7 instead of the requested August 31–September 6 period.

## Expected behavior and requirement

Weekly shipped units must use the agreed inclusive business-week boundaries.

## RCA and evidence

The customer directly identified the date-range mismatch. The correspondence does not identify a specific query defect or prove a recurring schedule correction.

## Documented actions

1. I received the corrected period from the stakeholder.
2. I supplied a revised DVUS Weekly Shipped Units by Location report using the requested dates.

## Outcome and remaining work

The replacement report was delivered. No claim is made about a permanent scheduling-code fix.

## Recommended validation and prevention

The following checks are proposed follow-up guidance unless explicitly confirmed above.

- Show the inclusive start and end dates in both the file and report header.
- Check timezone conversion and fiscal versus calendar-week definitions.
