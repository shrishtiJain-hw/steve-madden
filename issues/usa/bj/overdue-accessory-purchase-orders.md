# Accessories unavailable despite open purchase orders

**Country:** Usa\
**Brands:** BJ\
**Status:** Supply dependency identified; receipt pending\
**Contribution:** Investigation, validation, and stakeholder coordination

## Issue and business impact

Accessory styles had open purchase orders with arrival dates already in the past and no current inventory.

## Expected behavior and requirement

Preorder promises and availability should reflect actual supply status and current receipt expectations.

## RCA and evidence

My investigation found the POs remained open with zero inventory. The accessories team explained that warehouse transfers were still being arranged and dates had moved. No completed receipt or software fix was established.

## Documented actions

1. I checked the open POs, expected arrival dates, and inventory.
2. I requested confirmation of PO closure and incoming stock timing.
3. The business escalated transfer status to the accessories and warehouse teams.

## Outcome and remaining work

The supply dependency was identified and routed to its owners. The case remains open until receipt and availability are verified.

## Recommended validation and prevention

The following checks are proposed follow-up guidance unless explicitly confirmed above.

- Refresh expected dates from confirmed supply information.
- Verify receipt, inventory import, and preorder release as separate events.

## Evidence basis

My October 1 findings in “Betsey Accessories Hotwax Issue” and the accessories-team response.
